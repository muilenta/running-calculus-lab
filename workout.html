Units: the controls use minutes; graph time is in hours so slopes and areas work directly with mph. The runner's course shows miles. Section-by-section equations are below the charts.

The common pattern: x = x0 + v0u + ½au2; v = v0 + au; a is constant. Integration restores x0 and v0 as constants of integration. Since u differs from the overall time in hours only by a constant, differentiation with respect to either gives the same rates.

At the changes: position and velocity stay continuous. Acceleration changes abruptly in this idealized model; there is no single two-sided acceleration at the switching instants. Open circles show those instants rather than artificial ramps. At the start and finish, the displayed equations give the one-sided rates inside the run.

Clean numbers: all section times, target speeds, accelerations, and coefficients of u and u2 are integers. Starting positions need only tenths of a mile. Cruise speed is 8 mph (7:30/mile); both surges reach 10 mph (6:00/mile). Starting and stopping are the only slower sections.

Units: 12 mph/hour means a velocity increase of 1 mph every five minutes. All graph areas use hours. The full velocity area is exactly 10 miles; the full signed acceleration area is zero because the runner starts and finishes at rest.
